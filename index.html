Platform Features

Everything your 3PL needs

Purpose-built for third-party logistics providers who need power, speed, and reliability at scale.

Fleet Management

Manage your entire fleet from a single dashboard. Track vehicle health, assign loads, and monitor driver activity in real time.

Smart Route Planning

Manually assign or auto-optimize delivery routes. Reduce mileage, cut fuel costs, and improve on-time performance.

Live Delivery Tracking

Real-time GPS visibility for every shipment. Share live ETAs with customers and get notified on every status change.

Analytics & Reports

Deep-dive into performance metrics — on-time rates, cost per mile, driver scores, and custom KPI dashboards.

Alerts & Notifications

Automated alerts for delays, missed stops, driver idling, and route deviations. Stay ahead of every issue.

Compliance & Safety

Built-in ELD compliance, HOS tracking, and safety scorecards to keep your fleet audit-ready at all times.

Proof of Delivery

Capture electronic signatures, photos, and timestamps at every stop. Dispute-proof your deliveries automatically.

Instant Dispatch

Dispatch drivers instantly via mobile app. No phone calls, no paperwork — just one-tap job assignment.

How It Works

From order to delivery in 4 steps

01

Create Shipment Orders

Input pickup and delivery details, cargo specs, and priority levels. Import bulk orders via CSV or API integration.

02

Assign Drivers & Routes

Dispatchers assign the right driver to each order manually or let the system suggest the optimal assignment.

03

Track Live Progress

Monitor every vehicle on a live map. Get real-time ETAs and instant alerts for delays or deviations.

04

Confirm & Analyze

Capture proof of delivery, close jobs, and analyze performance data to continuously improve operations.
